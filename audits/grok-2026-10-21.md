## Threat model

Carrel is the private writing hub. The repo holds the app. The prose lives in the private repo `DrDustinEdwards/writing` and on the sites Carrel publishes through site-api. Two hostnames, chosen in `workers/app.ts`:

- `carrel.dustinedwards.info` is the browser door. Cloudflare Access runs first. `workers/gate.ts` verifies the Access JWT, loads the person, and refuses a reviewer. Pages are React Router.
- `carrel-mcp.dustinedwards.info` is the AI door, outside Access by a hostname bypass. It serves `/health`, OAuth metadata, `/authorize`, `/callback`, `/token`, `/register`, `/mcp`, and `/csp-report`. It does not serve the app pages.

Trust changes at the Access JWT, at the OAuth token's audience (`door.ts` requires `mcpOrigin + /mcp`), and at which `AGENT_KEY_*` secret a bearer matched. After that, one role table (`app/lib/roles.ts`) decides. A scope can narrow a role. It cannot widen one. Publish, send-external, delete, mentions, and manage are the Owner's. An agent row flagged Owner is refused.

Crons: `*/15 * * * *` (health, site refresh, Google refresh, inbox, social) and `17 3 * * *` (the D1 dump alone). No queue consumer and no email handler in the Worker export. Email is a binding the publish path uses to send.

STRIDE, short: spoofing is the Access JWT, the OAuth audience check, and the agent-key match. Tampering of a live post or an external send is the Owner role, checked again against the live item. Repudiation is the authorship row, `ai_publications`, and the MCP call log. Disclosure of the writing is Access on the browser host, and the AI door's `/health` returns only `ok` and `sha`. Denial of service is the MCP memory advisory for a caller who already has a grant or an agent key, plus unbounded CSP report logs and fetches with no timeout. Elevation is blocked by the role table, the Owner flag on an agent, and the refusal to add a second Owner.
